FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Talk to a human. We reply in minutes during market hours.

Is AbstractFigs a broker?

No. AbstractFigs is software. Your funds stay with your broker, and orders go straight to them through their official APIs. We never hold client money.

Which brokers can I connect?

89 retail and institutional brokers across forex, equities, ETFs, mutual funds, futures and options, with new integrations shipping every month. If yours is missing, tell us and we'll prioritize it.

Can I trade the same symbol across multiple accounts?

Yes. Pick the destination account in the ticket, set a default per market, or use smart routing. Positions from every account appear in one unified book.

Is market data included?

Paid plans include real-time data for forex and global index CFDs. Exchange-licensed equity and futures data can be added per exchange, or streamed straight from your connected broker.

How are my broker credentials stored?

Where brokers support OAuth, we never see your password. Otherwise tokens are encrypted with per-user keys in a hardware security module and never logged. We're SOC 2 Type II audited annually.

Does it run on desktop and mobile?

Native apps for macOS, Windows and Linux, plus a full web app and companion apps for iOS and Android. Layouts, drawings and alerts sync instantly.
