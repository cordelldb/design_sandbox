FAQ

Questions writers ask before uploading.

BasicsWhat is script coverage?

Script coverage is the written evaluation a reader prepares before a screenplay moves up the chain. It includes a logline, a synopsis, notes on story, character and structure, and a verdict: pass, consider or recommend. Agencies, studios, managers and contests use it to decide which scripts get read next.

PricingHow much does script coverage cost?

Traditional coverage commonly runs $75–$150 per script. A full Quilty feature analysis is $49.99, and plans start at $39.99/month, billed monthly, for two analyses. See pricing.

SecurityIs my script safe?

Yes. Your upload travels over TLS, and both the manuscript and the original file are encrypted again before they’re stored — authenticated encryption, with keys kept apart from database backups. Coverage providers see your script only to write your report, and they don’t train on it. You retain full ownership.

BasicsHow is this different from a human reader?

Quilty reads with the rigor and vocabulary of a seasoned development executive, but delivers in about an hour instead of weeks, at a fraction of the cost. Many writers use both: Quilty for fast iteration, human readers for final passes.

The reportWhat format is the report?

Coverage you read in your Quilty account, laid out the way studio readers write it. A full analysis includes the verdict, logline, story and character notes, market fit, the Quilty Score, and chapters on audience, market, money and production. Download it as a PDF anytime. Personal and Slate include full analyses each month, and a single analysis is available on its own.

The reportCan I share it with producers?

Absolutely — the report is yours. Many writers attach it to query letters and pitch decks as a credibility signal.

The AIHow accurate is the AI?

Quilty is calibrated against industry coverage standards. It won’t tell you what you want to hear — and that’s the point. Reads are honest, specific and consistently formatted.

The reportWhat file types do you accept?

PDF, Final Draft (.fdx), Word (.docx) and plain text (.txt), up to 50 MB.
