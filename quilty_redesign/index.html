Script coverage, explained

The read that decides what gets read next.

Coverage is the written evaluation a reader prepares before a screenplay moves up the chain: a logline, a synopsis, notes on story, character and structure, and a verdict — pass, consider or recommend. Agencies, studios, managers and contests rely on it to decide which scripts earn a closer look.

Traditional coverage typically costs $75–$150 and takes one to two weeks. Quilty delivers the same industry-standard format in about an hour — and shows the reasoning behind every note. It’s built for film, TV and stage writers, and for the producers who buy their work.

FAQ

Questions writers ask before uploading.

Can’t find what you need? Our team is happy to help at support@quilty.app.

What is script coverage?

Script coverage is the written evaluation a reader prepares before a screenplay moves up the chain. It includes a logline, a synopsis, notes on story, character and structure, and a verdict: pass, consider or recommend. Agencies, studios, managers and contests use it to decide which scripts get read next.

How much does script coverage cost?

Traditional coverage commonly runs $75–$150 per script. A full Quilty feature analysis is $49.99, and plans start at $39.99/month, billed monthly, for two analyses. See pricing.

Is my script safe?

Yes. Your upload travels over TLS, and both the manuscript and the original file are encrypted again before they’re stored — using authenticated encryption, with keys kept separate from database backups. Coverage providers see your script only to write your report, and they don’t train on it. You keep full ownership.

How is this different from a human reader?

Quilty reads with the rigor and vocabulary of a seasoned development executive, but delivers in about an hour instead of weeks, at a fraction of the cost. Many writers use both: Quilty for fast iteration between drafts, and human readers for the final pass.

What format is the report?

Your coverage lives in your Quilty account, laid out the way studio readers write it. A full analysis includes the verdict, logline, story and character notes, market fit, the Quilty Score, and chapters on audience, market, money and production. Download it as a PDF anytime. Personal and Slate include full analyses every month, or you can buy a single analysis on its own.

Can I share it with producers?

Absolutely — the report is yours. Many writers attach it to query letters and pitch decks as a signal of credibility.

How accurate is the AI?

Quilty is calibrated against industry coverage standards. It won’t tell you what you want to hear — and that’s the point. Every read is honest, specific and consistently formatted.

What file types do you accept?

PDF, Final Draft (.fdx), Word (.docx) and plain text (.txt), up to 50 MB.
